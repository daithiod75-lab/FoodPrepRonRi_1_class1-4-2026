Target Grammar (文法目標): Expressing abilities and skills with can and be able to (能力・「〜ができる」の表現方法).

Grammar 1 Expressing Ability with "can" (助動詞 can 「〜できる」)

Use can + [verb base form / 動詞の原形] to talk about skills or abilities.

Positive (肯定文) I can cook curry.
(私はカレーを作ることができます。)
Negative (否定文) I cannot (can't) cook fish.
(私は魚を調理することができません。)
Question (疑問文) Can you cook pasta? — Yes, I can. / No, I can't.
(あなたはパスタを作ることができますか？)
Grammar 2 Expressing Ability with "be able to" (be able to の使い方)

be able to + [verb base form] expresses capability. The be verb changes according to the subject (主語).

Subject (主語) Be Verb (be動詞) Form + Verb Example Sentence (例文)
I am able to cook I am able to make soup. (私はスープを作れます。)
You / We / They are able to cook We are able to use the stove. (私たちはコンロが使えます。)
He / She / It is able to cook She is able to bake bread. (彼女はパンが焼けます。)
